About

From breaking systems to building them.

I'm an AI Quality Engineer with 4 years of experience spanning automation testing, software development, and testing AI agents & LLM-powered systems. Currently a Senior SDET at OpenText, previously at RedBus (MakeMyTrip Group) and Mindtree.

My career started in hardcore test engineering — Selenium grids, API frameworks, CI/CD pipelines, performance testing. That discipline taught me exactly how software breaks. As LLMs entered production systems, I moved to the frontier: evaluating AI agents for output accuracy, hallucination, tool-use reliability, and edge-case behavior — and building AI-powered tooling of my own.

That's my differentiator: I understand how AI systems are built and how they break. Most engineers pick a side — I bridge both, applying QA rigor (evaluation frameworks, quality gates, automation) to the non-deterministic world of agents, RAG pipelines, and prompt chains.
